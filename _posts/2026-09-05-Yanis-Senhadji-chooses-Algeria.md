**FIFA has announced that former Spain U19 international Yanis Senhadji has officially changed his international allegiance from Spain to Algeria.**

The 21-year old striker, a product of the Real Betis academy, was part of Spain's U19 European championship winning squad in 2024. Born in the Catalonia region of Spain, Senhadji spent last season on loan with Tenerife and Hercules before joining Spanish 3rd division club Real Jaén this summer.
